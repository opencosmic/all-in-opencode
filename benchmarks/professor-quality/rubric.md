# Professor quality rubric

Evaluate each completed main-case report on a 0–5 scale for each dimension.
Keep a short evidence note for every score; objective runtime metrics are
context, not a substitute for report review.

| Dimension | 0–1 | 2–3 | 4–5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bounded completion | interrupted, lost state, or cannot reach a report | completes with avoidable dead work or weak closure | completes with durable state and preserves closure capacity |
| Question coverage | misses a requested dimension | covers the main question but leaves material gaps | covers each criterion and states residual gaps |
| Evidence atomicity | merged or uncited propositions | mostly bounded evidence with some broad records | each evidence record is one source-bounded proposition with a local span/context |
| Claim accounting | verdicts or uncertainty are absent/misleading | verdicts exist but uncertainty accounting is incomplete | every reportable claim has an honest supported/partial/unsupported/contradicted status |
| Report usefulness | unsupported conclusion or unusable citations | useful but ambiguous scope/version/source authority | direct answer, source-aware synthesis, contradiction handling, and actionable limits |

For the four blind cases, assign only PASS, PARTIAL, or FAIL after reading the
preserved report and state record:

- B1b: current versus legacy/version-specific Next.js behavior;
- B2b: released, roadmap/proposal, closed/open PR, third-party, and not-found
  distinctions;
- B3b: first-party authority versus community or third-party packaging;
- B4b: current/classic/rewrite/roadmap architecture separation and
  contradiction handling.

Record before/after assessments in a dated comparison note. Do not treat
fewer searches or fetches as an improvement if report quality regresses.
